## 3. Information Processed By the App

The App processes only what is needed to operate. We minimize collection.

### 3.1 Account
- Email address (Firebase Authentication)
- Display name
- Profile picture (optional, encoded image)
- Country and language preference

### 3.2 Data You Voluntarily Enter
- Sobriety records (start/end dates, optional notes)
- Drink entries (type, quantity, date)
- Body metrics (weight, unit preference)
- Milestones derived from your records
- Optional monthly spending and currency for the money-saved feature

### 3.3 Social Features
- Friend connections (user IDs)
- Friend requests
- Community messages (when the feature is enabled). Anything you post may be read by other users.

### 3.4 Subscription State
- A single boolean indicating whether your account currently has an active premium subscription. Payment cards are **never** seen or stored by us — Google Play Billing handles all payment data.

### 3.5 Diagnostics
- Crash reports via Firebase Crashlytics (stack traces, device model, OS version, app version)
- We do **not** collect device location, contacts, microphone, camera (unless you tap the in-app "Take a Photo" action), or device identifiers beyond what Firebase / AdMob require.

### 3.6 Advertising (free tier)
- The free tier shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect a device advertising ID and limited diagnostic data under **its own policy** at https://policies.google.com/privacy. You consent to this by using the free tier.
- Premium subscribers see no ads.
