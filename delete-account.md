# Delete your account — Alcohol Tracker

You can delete your Alcohol Tracker account and all associated data in two ways:

## In-app (recommended)

1. Open the app and sign in.
2. Go to **Settings → Delete account**.
3. Confirm.

This permanently removes your profile, sobriety records, daily check-ins,
drink entries, body metrics, friendships, friend requests, FCM token,
language preference, and notification preferences. A server-side function
cleans up edges held by other users (your entry in friends' lists, etc.).

## By email request

If you can't access the app, email us at:

**gyula.tasnadi.1990@gmail.com**

Subject: `Account deletion request`

Include the Google email used to sign in. We will delete your account
and all associated data within 30 days.

## Data retained after deletion

- Anonymous reference identifiers in other users' caches (no personal data,
  not linkable to you once your account record is gone).
- Crash and analytics reports retained by Google Firebase under their
  policy (typically 90 days, anonymized).
- Backup snapshots rotated within 30 days.
